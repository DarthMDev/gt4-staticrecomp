  Automatic + field mode (FFMD) = motion-adaptive weave; the presented field is the inverted CSR FIELD copied at the
  EE's vsync post (FIELD flips about 3.5 scanlines later).

- Fix: SetGsCrt posts SYNCV (NTSC/PAL, GS manual timings); protocol 1.1 (VSYNC field = display field, same values);
  gt4-gs 0.3.2 uses the record's own field. Published `gt4gs-v0.3.2` (local release, downloaded back, identical).
