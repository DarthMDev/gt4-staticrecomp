Every caller of the decrypt `func_00579E00`, via `func_0044D278`, and of the encrypt `func_00579F38`, from the

Attract or title demos, the showroom, photo mode and arcade demos have no decrypt call site. Oracle with the donor FPU

same FPU code. Replays and ghosts from a real PS2 card fail the CRC and are refused by `0x0044D1B8` (clean error, no
