# Journal 2026-10-22 (TASK-027, CoreGS 2.0)

### Owner test 3 and the follow-up

- The owner saw the License Center 3D car at a locked 60 FPS and a Time Trial at 39-42 FPS, lower when driving into
  the sun. The window also showed the 16:9 picture squeezed into 4:3.
- Disk: about 10.7 GB of old local intermediates deleted from the worktree; the references the checks use are kept.
- The window now shows the full 16:9 picture (IN-356); a test checks the rectangle.
- The slowdown into the sun and at night came from the lens-flare readback: every time the game read back its small
  flare table, the renderer copied all of its GPU targets back to the CPU, one at a time, waiting each time. Now it
  only copies what the game reads, in one step, and keeps the GPU fed during the frame (IN-357/IN-358). The flare
  values are byte-identical to before. Headless night race: 20-28 -> 11-19 ms per frame.
- GPU time can now be measured per category (`GT4_HW_GPU_TIMING=1`): about 8-9 ms per night frame, so the remaining
  cost of the heaviest frames is on the CPU (the GS command parser).
