  VU1 work onto the GPU and renders natively, opt-in, with fidelity as a measured target and effects restored one by
  one. A faithful renderer stays the default; game logic never changes. AGENTS.md scope updated (a native renderer
  was out of scope before); COREGS2-ROADMAP gains the CoreGS 3.0 section and the launcher gate for CoreGS 2.0.

## Frame cost, runtime side (TASK-084)

- Study section 6 instrumentation: pacing-sleep scope; the fixed critical-path formula (frame wall minus pacing
  sleep) in F8 and in the GS stream OSD; a `[frame]` section with the run-loop split (`run_due_events`,
  `kernel_service`, `guest_idle`, `syscall_dispatch`), syscalls by number with their wall time, thread switches,
  GIF bytes/records per path, VIF1 packets/jobs/bytes, DMA chains and the StreamSink walk time; `[mtvu]` doorbell
  metrics (rings, waits, wake-ups) and the bytes the EE forwards from the worker ring.
- Item 2: `VifBatch` chain jobs. The EE posts one job per VIF1 DMA chain at the chain end, at a stall, or before
  any journal or sync operation; per-direction doorbells with a sleeper count, and the producer waits for
  "worker progress" (output data or freed input space). Day race: doorbell rings 11,283 -> 350 per frame,
  `vif_gif` 1.31 -> 0.91 ms, frame wall -1.0 ms (table in BUILD.md).
- Item 4: with `GT4_GIF_FASTPATH` the GS stream skips the second walk of PATH2/PATH3 (their signals were applied
  when the packet was accepted) and the journal forwards whole worker records straight from the ring
  (`try_peek`/`release`), removing the per-record `std::vector` copy.
- Item 3: with `GT4_MTVU_VBLANK_JOURNAL` the VSYNC becomes a journal entry after the frame's output: the client
  holds it (`set_vsync_deferral`) and the drain posts it (`post_pending_vsync`). Verify mode and donor mode keep
  the blocking drain.
- Derivations: `--frame-cost` (runtime capsule) and `--frame-counters` (license guard) are opt-in, so every
  earlier capsule still derives byte for byte; `tests/python/test_frame_cost.py` checks the new hooks, the pin
  scripts and the harness schedules. New pins: `docs/inputs/slice260-instrument-runtime.json`,
  `slice260-license-guard-runtime.json`, `slice260-pcsx2.json`, `slice262-frame-cost-runtime.json`,
  `slice262-license-guard-runtime.json`, `slice262-pcsx2.json`.
- Checks: frame dumps and GS stream session hash identical to release 1.2.0 (slice240) on menus, day race and
