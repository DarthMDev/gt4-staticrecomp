# GT4Recomp 1.1.0

**Ultrawide and any aspect ratio.** Gran Turismo 4 now fills your screen at 21:9, 32:9, 16:10 and other shapes, taken automatically from the resolution you choose. You need your own disc image of the game.

## Install or update

- New install: download **GT4Recomp-Setup-1.1.0.exe** and run it (per-user, no administrator rights). On the first start the launcher asks for your disc image, copies and checks it, and downloads the PCSX2 GS renderer (gt4-gs 1.1.0).
- Update from 1.0.0: download and run the new installer over the old one. The update button of launcher 1.0.0 does not work (it closes without installing; fixed in 1.1.0, so later updates install from the launcher). Your settings, saves, replays and the copy of your disc image are kept; settings are moved to the new layout automatically (a backup is made first).
- The launcher now needs **gt4-gs 1.1.0**. If an older gt4-gs is installed, the launcher updates it at the next start (until then, the old one keeps working without the ultrawide fill).

## What's new

- **Automatic ultrawide and any aspect ratio.** The shape of the picture follows the output resolution; there is no aspect setting to get wrong.
  - Races fill the screen: wider screens see more to the sides (Hor+), taller screens see more above and below (Vert+). At 16:9 the picture is exactly as before.
  - The race HUD (dials, lap times, map, mirror) stays the game's 16:9 layout, undistorted and centred.
  - Menus, movies and photo mode stay 16:9, pillarboxed.
- **Monitor and Resolution.** Choose the monitor the game opens on (the primary one by default) and one of its real resolutions (the desktop resolution by default). Fullscreen stays borderless on that monitor.
- **Automatic internal resolution.** The game is drawn at the smallest whole multiple of the PS2 picture that covers your resolution (for example 3x at 1920x1080, 4x at 2560x1440, 6x at 3440x1440 and 3840x2160).
- **Render scale 50-200%.** Trade sharpness for speed in 25% steps; the row shows the result, for example "100% · 3x · 1920x1344".
- **gt4-gs 1.1.0**: the PCSX2 GS renderer can stretch the race view to the screen, and it always crops the 1-pixel line GT4 leaves on the right and bottom edges.
- **Settings > Graphics** now holds everything about the picture, in three sections: **Display** (Monitor, Resolution, Fullscreen, VSync), **Graphics** (Renderer, Render scale, anti-aliasing, sharpening) and **Advanced**. The separate Display tab is gone.

## Fixes

- **Updates from the launcher.** Accepting an update downloaded the setup, closed the launcher and installed nothing: Windows ended the setup together with the launcher. The setup now runs on its own, and the launcher refuses to update while the game is running.

## Known issues

- Split screen and photo mode have had little testing at ultrawide ratios (they stay 16:9, pillarboxed).
- Menus are still interlaced at 480i; progressive menus come in a later update.
- Some license demos and menu backgrounds do not play (the same as PCSX2 with default settings).
- Racing wheels are not supported yet.
- The installer and the launcher are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on the first start ("More info", then "Run anyway").
- Most testing was on an AMD GPU; please report problems with the launcher log (Settings > About > Open log folder).

## Requirements

Unchanged from 1.0.0: Windows 10 or 11 64-bit, a processor with SSE4.1, a graphics card with Vulkan 1.2, your own Gran Turismo 4 USA v2.00 (SCUS-97328) disc image as a plain .iso, and about 6 GB free.
