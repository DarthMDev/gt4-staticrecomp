# GT4Recomp 1.3.0

**Gran Turismo 4 and Gran Turismo 4 Spec II side by side, with a launcher you can carry.**

## New
- Gran Turismo 4 Spec II 1.10 joins Gran Turismo 4: each game has its own disc, saves and replays; settings and textures are shared.
- A game selector on the home screen; the game you played last is selected at start.
- "What's new" shows the notes of the installed version.
- The launcher follows the Windows language (English and Brazilian Portuguese), and General > Language lets you choose.
- Graphics API choice for GSdx: Vulkan (recommended), Direct3D 12 (recommended), Direct3D 11 for older PCs.
- Portable build: unpack the zip anywhere, your data stays in the folder.
- The game can also be started directly, without the launcher.

## Fixes
- Spec II's Options menu no longer closes the game.

## Good to know
- Gran Turismo 4 Spec II is a mod by TheAdmiester and contributors; full credits in About. GT4Recomp ships no Spec II files: you need your own Spec II 1.10 disc image.
- Your v2.00 saves and settings are kept when you update.
